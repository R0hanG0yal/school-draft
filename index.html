About the school

From a pathshala in 1901 to a CBSE school of today

Estd. 1901 CBSE Affiliation #1730009 100-Acre Campus Classes I to XII Boys

Birla School Pilani is the pioneer and oldest Birla educational institution in Pilani. It began in 1901 as a pathshala started by Seth Shiv Narayanji Birla for the education of his grandsons, Ghanshyam Das Birla and Rameshwar Das Birla.

The school grew through the twentieth century under the patronage of the Birla Education Trust. It was raised to an English middle school in 1916, became a high school in 1925, and added an intermediate college in 1928. In 1943 the university classes were separated from the school to form Birla College (now BITS Pilani).

After being known as Birla High School and Birla Senior Secondary School, the school took its present name in 2010. Today, it stands as an elite all-boys residential sanctuary of learning, affiliated to CBSE since 1985.

Historic Roadmap

125 Years of Defining Milestones

Key moments that built an enduring legacy of nation-building, scholarship, and discipline.

1901

Founding of the Pathshala

Seth Shiv Narayanji Birla lays the foundation stone for one of India's earliest residential learning centres in Pilani.

1916

English Middle School

Upgraded into an English-medium middle school, introducing modern science, mathematics, and global languages.

1925

Recognised High School

Formally accredited as a full-fledged High School, broadening matriculation across Rajasthan and northern India.

1928

Intermediate College

Intermediate college inaugurated under Ghanshyam Das Birla's vision to nurture nation-building leadership.

1943

Separation of Birla College

University faculties bifurcated to establish Birla College, which subsequently laid the foundation for BITS Pilani.

1985

CBSE Affiliation #1730009

Formal national affiliation granted under the Central Board of Secondary Education for competitive excellence.

2010

Birla School Pilani

Renamed to Birla School Pilani, inaugurating 21st-century smart classrooms, sports complexes, and equestrian facilities.

Academics

Classes I to XII under CBSE

The school follows the CBSE curriculum from the primary classes through to the board examinations, and prepares students for the Class X and Class XII examinations in the Science, Commerce and Humanities streams.

Science

Physics, Chemistry, Biology and Mathematics, with computer science as an elective.

Commerce

Accountancy, Business Studies and Economics.

Humanities

History, Political Science, Geography and other electives offered by the school.

Boarding life

Houses, study hours and daily routine

24/7 Pastoral Care Resident House Masters Hygienic Refectory Medical Infirmary

Residential students live on campus in heritage boarding houses, each nurtured under the direct mentorship of resident house masters, assistant house masters, and supportive superintendents.

The structured daily cadence balances rigorous academic classes, supervised evening study preps, inter-house sports tournaments, and enriching cultural societies.

Wholesome, dietitian-planned vegetarian meals are served in the school refectory, while a dedicated 24-bed on-campus infirmary with medical practitioners ensures around-the-clock healthcare. Boarders from across India and abroad build lifelong brotherhood and character here.

Admissions 2026-27

How to apply

Admissions are open for Classes V to IX and XI for the 2026-27 session, for both boarding and day scholars. Registration can be completed online, and assessments are also conducted on campus.

  1. 01

    Register

    Register online through the admission portal, or visit the school office on campus between 9:00 am and 5:00 pm.

  2. 02

    Assessment and interaction

    Appear for the aptitude assessment in English, mathematics and general aptitude, followed by a meeting with the Principal and house masters.

  3. 03

    Confirmation

    On selection, complete the admission formalities and pay the fees through the official payment gateway.

Register online Admission enquiry Portal login

Fees. The current fee structure, prospectus and hostel details are shared by the admissions office on request. Fees are payable only through the official payment gateway, the school mobile app, or by demand draft.

Help. The admissions helpline is open Monday to Saturday, 8:30 am to 5:00 pm, at +91 8094012101 and WhatsApp.
