Leadership • Sportsmanship • Expression

Co-Curricular & Sports

From the prestigious Horse Riding Academy and ceremonial Brass Band to National Cadet Corps (NCC) and robotics, co-curricular life at Birla School Pilani shapes champions of character.

Signature Discipline

Horse Riding Academy & Sports Arena

Birla School Pilani takes immense pride in maintaining one of India's few premier public school equestrian riding clubs, with well-bred thoroughbred horses, trained syces, and specialized riding coaches.

Our expansive sports grounds host national and IPSC tournaments in cricket, football, athletics, lawn tennis, basketball, badminton, swimming, and martial arts.

Holistic Leadership

Finishing School, DCIM & Governance

Finishing School

Comprehensive grooming in international etiquette, diplomacy, public speaking, interview mastery, and dining decorum.

DCIM & Technology

Digital Creativity, Innovation & Media center providing training in AI, robotics, film making, coding, and graphic arts.

Student Council

Democratic student government led by the Head Boy, Sports Captains, and House Prefects fostering executive responsibility.

Campus Traditions

National Cadet Corps (NCC) & Brass Band

The school boasts a distinguished NCC troop (Army & Naval wings) and an iconic student Pipe & Brass Band that regularly performs at state ceremonial parades and Republic Day celebrations.

  • Debating, Model United Nations, and Literary Society
  • Nature, Trekking, and Wildlife Conservation Club
  • Fine Arts, Classical Music, and Theatrical Society
  • Annual educational tours, heritage expeditions, and adventure camps

CBSE affiliated residential and day school in Pilani, Rajasthan. Founded 1901 by Seth Shiv Narayanji Birla.
