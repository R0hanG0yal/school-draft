Mentorship • Fellowship • Well-being

Pastoral Care & Boarding Life

A home away from home. Our residential houses cultivate brotherhood, self-discipline, and character under the 24/7 guidance of dedicated resident mentors.

Residential Life

Houses, Mentorship & Routine

At Birla School Pilani, boarding life is structured around historic houses where students from every state of India forge lifelong bonds of fellowship and mutual accountability.

From dawn physical training and morning assembly to supervised evening preparation and dining hall camaraderie, every hour is purposefully crafted for balanced mental, physical, and moral growth.

Pastoral Leadership

Care & Guidance Team

The Role of House Master

Senior faculty members residing adjacent to student dormitories, serving as pastoral guardians, academic mentors, and constant points of contact for parents.

Role of AHM & Tutors

Assistant House Masters and resident academic tutors supervise evening study hours, providing subject tutoring and pastoral counseling.

The Superintendent

Oversees residential facility management, housekeeping, hygiene protocols, laundry schedules, and campus round-the-clock safety.

Nutrition & Health

Refectory (Dining) & Infirmary

Our centralized school refectory serves four wholesome, nutritionally balanced vegetarian meals daily, planned by certified dietitians and prepared under rigorous standards of hygiene.

A full-time resident doctor and qualified nurses staff the school infirmary 24 hours a day, supported by the Birla Sarvajanik Hospital in Pilani for emergency care.

CBSE affiliated residential and day school in Pilani, Rajasthan. Founded 1901 by Seth Shiv Narayanji Birla.
