Academic Life

Affiliated to CBSE (No. 1730009). Fostering analytical clarity, scientific inquiry, and humanities scholarship from Class I to Class XII.

Senior Secondary (Classes XI & XII)

Specialised Academic Streams

Science Stream

Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology, Computer Science, and Engineering Graphics with hands-on research laboratory modules.

Commerce Stream

Accountancy, Business Studies, Economics, Applied Mathematics, and Informatics Practices designed for future economists and entrepreneurs.

Humanities Stream

History, Political Science, Geography, Psychology, and English Literature fostering critical reasoning and civil services preparation.

Research & Exploration

Knowledge Resource Center & Labs

The Knowledge Resource Center houses over 25,000 volumes, international educational journals, periodicals, and automated digital research stations.

  • Separate senior physics, chemistry, and biology research labs
  • Atal Tinkering and Robotics Innovation Center
  • Advanced Multimedia Language & Communication Lab
  • Supervised evening prep and silent study halls for boarders

Honours & Distinctions

Prizes, Scholarships & Academic Awards

BET Scholar Trophy

Conferred annually upon board examination toppers achieving exemplary percentile performance in CBSE Class X & XII.

Proficiency Awards

Subject gold medals recognizing exceptional mastery in mathematics, natural sciences, languages, and debate.

National Olympiad Honors

Birla School Pilani scholars consistently qualify in the Indian National Mathematical Olympiad, KVPY, and NTSE.
