Academic Session 2026-27

Admissions

Admissions are open for Classes V to IX and XI for the 2026-27 session. Welcome to a legacy of residential excellence and holistic development.

Step-by-Step Guide

Admission Process & Criteria

  1. 01

    Online Registration

    Complete the student profile on the official BET admission portal with previous academic marks and certificates.

  2. 02

    Assessment & Interaction

    Attend the entrance test assessing English, Mathematics, and General Aptitude, followed by an interaction with the Principal and house masters.

  3. 03

    Enrolment Confirmation

    Secure provisional admission, verify original documents, and complete fee payment via the BET payment gateway.

Fee Structure 2026-27 & 2025-26

Birla School Pilani maintains a fully transparent fee structure inclusive of tuition, boarding, laundry, dining, infirmary, sports academy, and academic prep.

Fee schedules for Classes V through XII (both boarding and day scholar options) can be downloaded directly or requested from the admissions office.

  • Annual, bi-annual, and quarterly payment terms
  • Authorized online payment gateway and UPI/Demand Draft modes
  • Merit scholarship consideration for exceptional academic and sports achievers
